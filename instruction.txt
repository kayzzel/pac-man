PAC-MAN - INSTRUCTIONS
======================

CONTROLS
--------
Arrow keys or WASD ........ move Pac-Man
ESC ....................... pause / resume the game

A move is applied when Pac-Man reaches the center of the next
cell: presses are queued, so you can steer slightly in advance.

GAME MODES
----------
Mandatory .... the classic run: a chain of 10 levels (minimum)
               of mazes generated by the A-Maze-ing package. The
               first maze always comes from the fixed seed (42),
               the following ones are random.
Arcade ....... the levels of the original 1980 Pac-Man,
               faithfully recreated: the same layouts, the same
               rules, the same scoring.
Custom maps .. your own mazes, built with the in-game map
               creator and selected from the map choice menu.

GAME RULES
----------
- Eat every pacgum of a level to complete it, and every level of
  the mode to win the game.
- Touching a ghost without the power-up costs one life; at zero
  lives the game is over. Positions reset and the game pauses
  after each death: you resume when you are ready.
- Each level has a countdown (90 seconds by default, see
  level_max_time). When it runs out, the game is lost, regardless
  of remaining lives. The clock only runs while you are actually
  playing: paused time is never counted.
- Score and remaining lives are kept between levels. The score
  never decreases during a game.
- At the end of a game (win or lose), the final score is shown,
  you can enter your name, and the top 10 scores are displayed in
  the main menu.

THE POWER-UP
------------
The super-pacgums (the big dots in the corners) are worth more
points and give the power-up for 7 seconds: the ghosts turn blue
and flee, and touching one eats it:
- eaten ghosts travel back to their spawn as a pair of eyes and
  reappear there;
- each ghost can only be eaten once per power-up;
- during the last 3 seconds the ghosts blink: the effect is about
  to end, finish the hunt quickly;
- eating a new super-pacgum while energized restarts the 7
  seconds and makes every ghost edible again.

THE GHOSTS
----------
BLINKY (red) .... follows Pac-Man directly.
PINKY (pink) .... aims 4 cells in front of your direction.
INKY (cyan) ..... mirrors a point calculated with Blinky.
CLYDE (orange) .. chases when far from you, retreats to its
                  corner when you are less than 8 cells away.

Ghosts alternate on a timed schedule between chasing you and
regrouping at their corners.

POINTS
------
Pacgum ............. 10 points (config: point_per_pacgum)
Super-pacgum ....... 50 points (config: point_per_super_pacgum)
Edible ghost ....... 200 points (config: point_per_ghost)

SCORES
------
- Each mode and each custom map keeps its own scores.
- The mandatory mode stores them in the file given by the config
  (highscore_filename, default "highscore.json"); every other map
  keeps a score file in its own folder.
- Your name is limited to 10 characters, letters, digits and
  spaces only.
- Only the top 10 scores are kept.

CUSTOM MAPS
-----------
Build your own mazes with the in-game map creator:

1. Name it: letters, digits, _ and - only, 20 characters max,
   and it must not already exist.
2. Pick the size (from 10x10 up to 30x30), then either generate
   a random maze or start from an empty room and draw the walls
   yourself. The outer border of the map cannot be pierced.
3. Place Pac-Man's spawn on an open cell.
4. Place the collectibles: every one of them must be reachable
   from Pac-Man's spawn.
5. Place the four ghosts' spawns (each must be able to reach
   Pac-Man) and their scatter corners.
6. Save: the map lands in its own folder, keeps its own scores,
   and becomes playable from the map choice menu.

A map with no collectible, an unreachable pacgum or a sealed
ghost spawn is refused, so everything you save is winnable.

OPTIONS (CHEAT PANEL)
---------------------
Available to facilitate the review / to play around:
- Invincible: ghosts cannot kill you.
- Clipping: move and turn through walls (never outside the maze).
- Add or remove lives (never below 1).
- Add or remove points (never below 0).

CONFIGURATION
-------------
The game takes exactly one argument, a configuration file:

    python3 pac-man.py config.json

The file is JSON with comments (lines starting with #, and //
are also supported). All keys are optional, invalid values fall
back to defaults, and unknown keys are ignored:

    highscore_filename ...... file of the mandatory game's scores
                              (default "highscore.json")
    nb_level ................. number of mandatory levels,
                              minimum 10 (default 10)
    levels_dimensions ........ [width, height] of the levels,
                              between 10 and 50 (default [15, 15])
    lives ..................... starting lives (default 3)
    point_per_pacgum .......... pacgum value (default 10)
    point_per_super_pacgum .... super-pacgum value (default 50)
    point_per_ghost ........... eaten ghost value (default 200)
    seed ...................... seed of the first level's maze
                              (default 42)
    level_max_time ............ seconds per level (default 90)

TIPS
----
- Corner super-pacgums are a panic button: save them for when
  ghosts surround you.
- Learn the regrouping windows: ghosts give you breathing time.
- In the blinking phase ghosts are still edible: do not back
  off too early.
- In custom maps, long corridors are deadly: leave intersections
  for the ghosts to make mistakes.

Good luck!
